YouTube Integration

"Bring your music with you."

Seamlessly import your favorite YouTube playlists into Auralis. You can connect your Google account or simply paste any public/unlisted playlist URL to enjoy continuous playback and synchronized lyrics.

Selective, User-Driven Import

You choose exactly which playlists to import into your personal Auralis library.

Strict Read-Only Permission

Auralis requests strictly youtube.readonly scope or reads public playlist metadata. We never modify your YouTube account.

Private & Decentralized Fallbacks

Built-in integration with trusted privacy-respecting instance pools (Piped & Invidious) ensures your streams remain resilient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions about Auralis

Got questions about how Auralis works, our permissions, or YouTube playlist import? Find answers here.

You can import playlists either by pasting any public or unlisted YouTube playlist link, or by connecting your Google account. Auralis uses read-only access (youtube.readonly) to fetch track names, artists, and public album art into your personal playlist library. Auralis never modifies your YouTube library or account.
Listen Together allows you to create a shared room with a unique 6-character code. Friends join using the code and their playback position, play/pause states, and queue automatically synchronize in real time using Cloud Firestore.
Lyrics are fetched dynamically from community-driven open lyrics databases such as LRCLIB, matching track titles and artists with line-by-line timecodes, plus fallback YouTube caption matching.
