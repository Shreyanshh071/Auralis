Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Overview

Auralis ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source, privacy-first audio and music streaming client. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Google & YouTube User Data

When you use our YouTube playlist import feature, Auralis requests strictly read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly) to retrieve your personal playlist metadata (playlist titles, track titles, artists, and thumbnails).

  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google or YouTube user data to any third parties or advertisers.
  • No Account Alteration: We never modify, delete, or create YouTube playlists or videos on your account.
  • Google Policy Compliance: Auralis strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

Your library, favorites, and custom playlists are stored locally on your device by default. If you choose to enable cloud backup via Firebase, your preferences are securely stored in your private, authenticated Firebase cloud account.

4. Data Deletion and Revocation

You may disconnect your Google Account at any time within the app or revoke access via Google Account Security Settings.
