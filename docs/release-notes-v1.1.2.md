# Auralis 1.1.2

The biggest Android update since 1.1.0: a new look across the app, three player designs, Auralis Connect, a guided first launch, and a long list of fixes.

## Added

### Liquid Glass

- **New in 1.1.2: Liquid Glass** — a see-through glass look for the dock, mini player, page buttons, sheets and menus. It works on dark themes only, so the toggle is greyed out in light mode. Turn it on in the tour or in Appearance.

### Player

- **Three player designs** — choose between New, Old and Immersive. Immersive has full-bleed artwork, auto-hiding controls and its own lyric fillers.
- **Animated covers** with several sources. They start once a song has been playing for two seconds, so swiping stays smooth.
- **Folia player backgrounds** — five new styles: Latent, Monet, Paper texture, Lens distortion and Sora.
- **Frosted mini player** — a new frosted blur background for the mini player.
- **Ambient player mode.**
- **Mini player design picker** and a Liquid Glass dock that minimises as you scroll.

### Lyrics

- **Spicy Lyrics** provider with its own native lyric style.
- **Lyrics source picker** — see where lyrics came from and choose another source. Providers with word-by-word timing are tried first.
- **Lyric preview in every player design**, with a lyric line in Immersive.

### Listening across devices

- **Auralis Connect** — one listening session across your devices. Hand playback between your phone and desktop. The PC app is coming soon.
- **Listening from your other devices appears live** in your stats, instead of waiting for the next sync.

### Listen Together

- **Guests can edit the queue**, with room activity showing who did what.
- **Listen Together popup** available from song menus.

### Getting started

- **Eight-page interactive app tour** with live previews of the real player, lyric styles and Home layout, plus audio quality, permissions and region and language choices.
- **Artist picker on first launch**, which builds a starter Home from the artists you choose.
- **Liquid Glass comparison** in the tour. Your choice is saved.
- **Animated logo intro** and a theme-aware launch screen that hands off to Home without a jump.

### Library and search

- **Artists section** in Library.
- **Save albums to your library** from the + buttons.
- **Optional Spotify catalog** with YouTube as a fallback.
- **Release cards in search**, with album and Single cards shown alongside the first results.

### Other

- **Resumable in-app updates** with their own screen.
- **Content settings** and app translations for Hindi, Bengali and Punjabi.
- **Disconnect confirmation** so an accidental tap doesn't sign you out.

## Redesigned

- **Settings** — every settings page has a new layout, for both Liquid Glass and normal mode.
- **Profile, Account, Discord, YouTube and Import** pages.
- **Artist, album and playlist pages, Library, History and Stats.**
- **Option sheets, dialogs and the sort menu**, in both Liquid Glass and normal mode.
- **Pull to refresh** — a floating button that fills as you pull, used across the whole app. The page no longer moves.
- **Page headers** — back buttons and titles line up the same way everywhere.
- **Library animations** — the search bar opens from its icon, and switching between grid and list is animated.
- **Status bar glow** on Home, Search and Library in Liquid Glass.

## Fixed

### Player and playback

- **Fast swiping** no longer leaves the cover, the background and the playing song out of step. Swiping past several songs used to reorder the queue.
- **One change per swipe stop** — swipe as much as you like. The song, colours and background change once, when you stop.
- **Laggy swiping** caused by animated covers.
- **Players fit every screen** without shrinking the cover.
- **Best free YouTube audio** is always picked, and dubbed tracks are skipped.
- **Featured and single versions** of a song stay distinct when they have the same length.
- **Offline mode** now really works, and pages reload by themselves when the network returns.
- **Mini player** honours your chosen background with Liquid Glass on, tucks smoothly into the dock and stays readable on light glass.

### Lyrics

- **Smooth lyric scrolling** is back after it stopped gliding between lines.
- **Lyrics load faster**, without waiting on slow providers.
- **Centring and scrolling** during playback no longer jump past sung lines.
- **Glow artifacts** on neighbouring words in Fluid and MetroLyrics.
- **Lyrics after seeking** re-sync to the right position.
- **Transliteration no longer forced** — lyrics show in their source script.

### Search

- **Partial titles** — the song you're typing now appears as you type, instead of only unrelated songs with one matching word.
- **Accurate ranking, real album labels and official artists**, with faster results.
- **A compilation is no longer treated as a song's home album.**
- **"Also matching"** only shows meaningful results, with no repeats below it.

### Library and playlists

- **+ buttons** on album and playlist pages now work.
- **Albums and artists open much faster**, using a cache that survives restarts.
- **Pinned section** shows only what you pinned. Liked Songs is no longer pinned by default.
- **Playlists are back in Speed Dial**, where songs were pushing them out.
- **Complete YouTube and Spotify playlist imports.**
- **One notification per playlist action**, instead of a pill and a toast.

### Accounts and sync

- **Your library and stats restore in one pass** on sign-in.
- **Listens synced while playing** grow to their full time.
- **Sign-out** clears local account state straight away instead of waiting on a cloud upload.
- **New sign-ins** enter the tour after authentication, and returning listeners skip it.
- **Screens no longer show through** account pages.

### Other

- **Discord status** now shows the song name on phones, as it does on PC.
- **Dim text** in Liquid Glass.
- **Lines on cards** in Settings and Profile.
- **Dimmed Home screen** in Liquid Glass.
- **Curly quotes** in Listen Together activity.
- **Theme & Colors** margins and cut-off text.

## Improved

- **Theme changes** crossfade smoothly.
- **Seeking across connected devices** — the latest position wins during repeated scrubbing.
- **Startup** — the logo stays until the first frame is ready.

## Defaults for new installs

- Liquid Glass is off and can be turned on in the tour or Appearance.
- Old player, New mini player and a Live Mesh mini player background.
- Swipe to remove a song from a playlist is off.
- Top, weekly-most-played and monthly-most-played automatic playlists start hidden.

These only apply when a preference hasn't been saved. Your existing choices aren't changed.
