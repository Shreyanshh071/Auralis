Spotify Canvas comes to the phone, lyrics pick better sources, and your library syncs on a faster service.

## New

- **Spotify Canvas** — songs with a Spotify Canvas play it behind the full player. The cover dissolves into the video, and it stays softly blurred behind Lyrics and Queue. Songs without one fall back to animated album covers, then the still cover.
- **Canvas settings like the PC** — Appearance now has **Animated covers** and **Canvas** sections, each with its own switch, plus a Wi-Fi only option.
- **Fetch Canvas with your Spotify account** — finds a Canvas for songs the public source doesn't have. If you're signed out, tapping it offers Spotify sign-in.
- **Lyric sharing** — share a lyric as a card.
- **New lyric styles** — the preview under the cover follows the style you pick.
- **More playlist imports** — Deezer, Tidal and Amazon Music, alongside an automatic library import.
- **Faster library sync** — your library moves to a faster sync service automatically the next time it syncs. Devices still on older versions keep syncing in the meantime.
- **Reduce effects** — lighter visuals for smoothness and battery, optionally switched on with battery saver.
- **Listening stats credit every artist** on a collaboration.

## Improved

- **Better lyric sources** — word-by-word lyrics from any source now beat line-by-line Spicy Lyrics. Spicy still leads when the timing is equal.
- **Spicy Lyrics finds more songs**, including songs without an album name, and songs that start singing in the first moment no longer lose their word sync.
- **Lyrics provider picker** says when a source's timing is for a different version of the song instead of "Lyrics not found".
- **Smoother lyric scrolling** and Spicy Lyrics' blur in every style.
- **Listen Together** rooms have a glass banner and glass queue confirmations, and each listener can have one waiting request.
- **Animated covers** load sooner and keep their videos.
- **AutoMix** lines up the next song before it becomes audible.
- **YouTube sign-in** stays fresh, and its sign-in page was fixed.
- Theme & Colors options sit in one glass card, Home menu icons match across themes, and viewing a song's artist opens the channel it links.

## Fixed

- **Overlapping Spicy lyrics** on repeated words and in complex scripts.
- **The active lyric line** no longer loses its first letters.
- **Queue**: no shuffle flash, Play next works on the playing song, swiping queue items works, and adding a song already up next says so instead of adding it twice.
- **Immersive player**: scrubbing no longer changes the song.
- **Listening history** records reliably and reaches your other devices.
- **AutoMix** analysis fixed, and it respects crossfade being turned off.
- Artists with "&" or a comma in their name ("She & Him", "Tyler, The Creator") open directly instead of being split into made-up pieces.
- The settings header fade on short pages.

## Changed

- Settings no longer sync between devices through your account.
- Five lyric styles were removed, and ViviMusic was renamed.
